Family feedback survey template+

Invite honest feedback, make participation optional, and explain who will read responses. Avoid collecting children’s sensitive information.

  1. How did you first hear about our program?
  2. How easy was it to find the information you needed? (1–5)
  3. Did our response help you understand your next step? (1–5)
  4. How welcome did you feel during your first conversation or visit? (1–5)
  5. What helped you feel confident about our program?
  6. Was anything unclear or harder than it needed to be?
  7. What is one thing we could improve about our communication?
  8. What would you tell another family considering our school?

Ask separately for permission before using any response as a public testimonial.

Local visibility review checklist+

Choose three nearby programs serving similar ages. Record what you observe and the date; this is a comparison worksheet, not an automated report.

  1. Search locally: which programs appear, and are your school’s contact details accurate?
  2. Compare clarity: ages served, hours, educational approach, and location.
  3. Look for trust signals: current photos, useful reviews, and clear staff information.
  4. Follow the inquiry path: can a parent easily find a phone number, form, or tour request?
  5. Check next-step expectations: does the website explain what happens after an inquiry?
  6. Choose one improvement that would make your own parent experience clearer.
Five ways AI can support your enrollment team+
  1. Capture: bring inquiries from agreed channels into one visible workflow.
  2. Clarify: answer approved routine questions and pass sensitive or uncertain ones to staff.
  3. Coordinate: connect families with your agreed tour scheduling process.
  4. Follow up: send approved reminders and helpful next steps with appropriate consent.
  5. Learn: review response times, tours, and drop-off points to guide improvements.

A few fair questions

Before you invite anyone into your enrollment process…

You should understand exactly what the system will—and will not—do.

Are you replacing our staff?+

No. The workflow handles acknowledgment and visibility. Your staff handles judgment, relationships, sensitive questions, and enrollment decisions.

Do we have to replace Procare, LineLeader, or our current system?+

Not automatically. We review what you already have before proposing an integration or change. Compatibility is confirmed during discovery. If the function already works well, you may not need us.

Is this an AI receptionist?+

No. The founding offer uses an approved workflow with human escalation. Open-ended voice or chat would require a separate scope and technical review.

What if we operate several locations?+

We agree on the locations and inquiry sources together. We can start there, measure the result, and discuss a separate scope for additional locations.

Will this increase enrollment?+

It is designed to improve a process that can influence tours and enrollment, but we do not guarantee enrollment or claim causation without evidence.

How much time will this take from our team?+

Plan for roughly two hours across kickoff, message and workflow approvals, acceptance, and training, assuming timely access and decisions.
